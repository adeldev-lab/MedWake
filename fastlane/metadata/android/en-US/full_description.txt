MedWake is a privacy-first, 100% offline medication reminder built around a simple idea: a dose becoming due should not wake you up. The alarm waits until you actually pick up and unlock your phone.

HOW IT WORKS
- At the scheduled time (for example 07:00) the dose becomes DUE. If the phone is locked or you are asleep, MedWake stays silent.
- The first time you unlock the phone after that, MedWake shows a full-screen alarm (with an optional 0-120 second delay).
- If you are already using the phone when the dose becomes due, the alarm rings right away.
- If you snooze, the replacement dose waits for its new time and then rings on your next unlock.

FEATURES
- Wake-gated full-screen alarm with Take now, Skip, Snooze and reschedule.
- The snooze length is configurable and is used by the alarm and notification actions.
- Gentle volume ramp-up from 5% to 100% over 5-30 seconds, so the alarm does not startle you.
- Monthly adherence calendar with taken, skipped and missed days, current streak and percentage.
- Flexible schedules: every day, weekdays, weekends, Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun, Tue/Thu/Sat or any custom day combination.
- Stock counts with a low-stock warning (∞ means stock is not tracked), and editable dose history.
- Five alarm themes plus a robot mascot or your own wallpaper.
- Backup and restore to a local JSON file.
- Optional quick-open catch-up and safety-net reminders.
- Works on Android 8.0+ (minSdk 26), optimized for Android 14-16 and Samsung One UI.

PRIVACY
- No internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics, no cloud.
- All data stays in the app's private database on your phone.
- Usage access is optional and is used only to confirm that you really unlocked the phone (keyguard events). It is not used to read which apps you use.
- Full privacy policy: https://github.com/adeldev-lab/MedWake/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

PERMISSIONS
MedWake asks for notifications, exact alarms, full-screen intents, battery optimization exemption and, optionally, usage access. Each one is explained in the app and in the privacy policy.

MedWake is a reminder tool, not a medical device and not medical advice. The operating system or the user can always prevent an alarm from firing. Keep a backup reminder for critical medication.
